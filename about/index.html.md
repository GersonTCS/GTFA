# Built by a local family, for local families.

We're not a staffing agency.

We're neighbors who got tired of wishing someone would just help.

## Our Story

For a long time, we were just managing. Dylan was working 48-hour shifts at the Fire Department. Erin was running her own business, seeing clients as a dietitian, and doing the mental math that comes with raising two kids. The house was always behind. Groceries, errands, the pile of things that kept getting pushed to next week.

​

We finally just hired someone to help, but we needed someone that didn't just watch the kids or clean the house. We needed someone to handle our to-do list and take off the mental load.

​

It was one of the best decisions we ever made. We got our time and peace if mind back.

​

We couldn't stop thinking about how many families in Georgetown needed exactly that and had no good way to find it. So we built it.​

​Why We're Different

We vet every assistant in person, run background checks, and only put people in homes we'd be comfortable putting in our own.

​

We also built this specifically for types of families - seniors who want to stay in their homes and keep their independence, and busy families who just need reliable help.

## Meet the Team

Dylan Kenny

Lieutenant / Paramedic + Co-Founder

Dylan has been in the fire and EMS world for over 15 years. He knows what it means to show up for people on their worst days, and he brings that same reliability to everything we do here. He's the face you'll see in the community, the one who takes the calls, and the reason clients feel like they're in good hands from the first conversation.

Erin Kenny

Registered Dietitian, Business Owner + Co-Founder

Erin is a registered dietitian and runs her own practice alongside GTFA. She's also a mom of two, which means she's lived the exact chaos this business was built to solve. She manages the day-to-day operations, client relationships, and makes sure the experience on your end actually feels like it was thought through, because it was.
