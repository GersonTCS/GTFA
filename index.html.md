## We are your village.

Georgetown Family Assistants was started by a local firefighter and his working wife - because we lived it ourselves.

Between shifts, work, kids, and everything else on the list, we kept wishing someone could just handle the stuff that piles up. Groceries. Errands. Getting the house in order. We didn't need a housekeeper or a full-time helper. We just needed a hand.

So we built what we couldn't find.

Every assistant we send is vetted, background-checked, and trained to show up and get things done. We treat every home like our own, because this started in ours.

## Questions? We've got answers.

## Still on the fence? Here's what most people ask before booking their first visit.

### What exactly happens during a 4-hour visit?

Your assistant shows up, works through whatever's on your list, and sends you a photo update when they're done. That's it. You tell us what you need most (groceries, a clean house, errands, meal prep) and we handle it. No micromanaging required.

### Who is coming into my home?

Every assistant goes through a background check before they ever step foot in a home. We're firefighter-owned and operated, and we hold our team to the same standard we'd want in our own house. We don't send anyone we wouldn't trust ourselves.

### Do I have to commit to a plan?

Nope. Your First Dispatch is a single visit with zero commitment. If you love it, you can move to a monthly or weekly plan.

### What if I need something that's not on the service list?

Just ask. The list covers the most common requests, but we're flexible. If it's safe, legal, and within reason, we'll do our best to make it happen. Call us and we'll talk through it.

### Can my assistant drive me to appointments?

Yes, appointment transportation is one of our most popular services, especially for seniors. Just let us know when you book and we'll make sure the visit is planned around it.

### How do I book?

Click the "Get Started" button and fill out a short form. You can also call us directly if you'd rather talk to a real person first. We mean it, call us.

### What areas do you serve?

We're based in Georgetown, TX and serve the greater Georgetown area, including Sun City. Not sure if we cover your neighborhood? Give us a call and we'll let you know.
