Over the Counter Medications

How to find inactive ingredients

The inactive ingredients will be listed on the packaging under the ‘active ingredients’ line.

You can also search online for product information if you don’t have the packaging.

Prescription Medications

How to find inactive ingredients

The inactive ingredients will be listed on the packaging, the patient information leaflet, or you can ask your pharmacist.

You can also use DailyMed to find information for each medication.
