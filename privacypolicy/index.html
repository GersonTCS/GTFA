Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need, we don't sell your information, and we treat your data the way we treat your home, with care and respect. The full details are below.

​

Who We Are

Georgetown Family Assistants ("GFA," "we," "us," or "our") is a locally owned and operated personal assistant service based in Georgetown, Texas. We provide in-home and errand-based services for families and seniors in the Georgetown area.

What Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways — directly from you, and automatically when you use our website.

Information you give us directly:

  • Your name

  • Phone number and email address

  • Home address or service location

  • Information about who the services are for (yourself or a family member)

  • Service preferences, scheduling requests, and any special instructions you provide

  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment provider — we do not store your card details)

  • Any other information you choose to share through our contact or booking forms

Information collected automatically: When you visit our website, we may automatically collect basic technical information including:

  • Your IP address

  • Browser type and device information

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site

  • Referring website or search term that brought you here

This information is collected through standard website analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Wix Analytics) and helps us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.

​

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and booking requests

  • Schedule and coordinate your service visits

  • Communicate with you about your appointments, updates, or changes

  • Process payments securely

  • Improve our website and services

  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

​

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Full stop.

We may share limited information with trusted third parties only when necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Payment processors — to securely handle billing and transactions

  • Scheduling or operations software — to manage appointments and communications

  • Website platform providers — our site is hosted on Wix, which has its own privacy practices you can review at wix.com/about/privacy

  • Legal authorities — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our clients, staff, or the public

Any third party we work with is required to handle your information responsibly and only for the purposes we specify.

​

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies (small text files stored on your device) to help the site function properly and to understand how it's being used.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to work (e.g., form functionality, navigation)

  • Analytics cookies — help us understand visitor behavior so we can improve the site (e.g., Google Analytics)

  • Marketing cookies — may be set by third-party tools if we run advertising campaigns (e.g., Facebook Pixel)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect how the site functions.

If you are visiting from the European Union or California, additional rights may apply. See Sections 8 and 9 below.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These include:

  • Secure HTTPS encryption on our website

  • Limited internal access to client information

  • Trusted, reputable third-party platforms for payments and communications

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we do everything we reasonably can to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

​

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy — or as required by law.

If you'd like us to delete your information, see Section 10 on how to contact us.

​

California Residents (Your Rights Under CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you and how it is used

  • Request deletion of your personal information

  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell data)

  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To make a request, contact us using the information in Section 10. We will respond within 45 days.

​

European Users (Your Rights Under GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Request correction of inaccurate data

  • Request deletion of your data

  • Object to or restrict how we process your data

  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10.

Our lawful basis for processing your information is legitimate interest (operating and improving our services) and, where applicable, your consent or the performance of a contract.
